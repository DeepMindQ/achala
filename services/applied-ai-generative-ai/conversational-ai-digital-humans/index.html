Containment and trust are one metric, not two

Measured as a pair per intent family, divergence triggers policy rework, not threshold moves

Conversational AI & Digital Humans is delivered by an Achala practice team that stays through industrialization. Achala ships applied AI with its evidence attached: evaluation harnesses, drift monitoring and measured deltas against your baseline. If a model does not beat the baseline, we say so — that honesty is why clients keep the programs running. Who this is for: product and business leaders with a P&L line to move, and CDOs consolidating AI governance.

Digital humans add a presentation layer, not a magic one. The avatar succeeds when the conversation underneath is grounded, the handoffs are dignified, and the voice stack holds latency budgets on real networks.

What follows is the build anatomy: the failure modes that inflate containment while destroying CSAT, the architecture that holds both, and the console that keeps the trade visible every day.

Dialog governance — consent, guard decisions, trace retention

The audit trail a regulated channel carries

Dialog traces are retained with consent state, guard decisions and handoff context attached, the audit path a communications regulator would ask for, already assembled.

  • Advisability guard

    Regulated advice topics refuse and route; refusal text cites policy

  • Consent & recording rules

    Voice recording consent enforced per jurisdiction before ASR retains

  • PII minimization

    Card and identifier masking in transcripts and logs by default

  • Human override

    Agents can seize sessions; the action is logged and reviewed weekly

Every guard intervention is reviewable as a transcript pair, the turn attempted and the grounded alternative served, so policy tuning is evidence-led, not anecdote-led.

Escalation is the moment the product is judged

Dignified handoff design, what the human sees, and why containment without it is a churn machine

Containment numbers collapse in board reviews because they hide the escalation: a session the bot 'contained' but the customer experienced as stonewalling shows up later as churn, complaint volume and agent handle-time inflation. We therefore design the handoff as a first-class surface, the agent desktop receives the transcript, the detected intent, the customer's history and the bot's confidence, and the dialog policy chooses the handoff wording, never dumping the customer into a queue with 'transfer' as the last message. Containment and CSAT are measured as a pair per intent family; a family that gains containment while losing CSAT gets its policy rewritten, not its threshold raised.

The guard chain runs before generation: topic bounds, PII filters and injection defenses are pre-generation filters, not post-hoc apologies. Barge-in and VAD tuning are treated as first-class engineering, voice containment dies at double-detection latency, and the dialog eval suite replays real transcript samples every release, because dialog quality drifts with every prompt edit.

  • Dignified handoff by design

    Transcript, intent, history and confidence reach the human, never a bare transfer.

  • Containment × CSAT, paired

    Measured per intent family; policy rewritten when the pair diverges.

  • Pre-generation guard chain

    Topic, PII and injection filters run before tokens are produced.

  • Voice engineering as first-class

    Barge-in, VAD and latency budgets tuned like any other p95.

  • How do you stop the bot from stonewalling customers?

    Every intent family carries a containment band signed with the contact-center owner; when containment pushes above band while CSAT drops, policy rework is triggered, divergence between the two numbers is the alarm, not a success.

  • What does a dignified handoff actually look like?

    The customer never repeats themselves: the handoff carries full context, the reason for escalation and the trace to the agent desktop. The human sees what was tried and why it failed, that is the product moment, engineered deliberately.

  • Can the assistant answer from our regulated documents safely?

    Yes, answers are grounded in retrieval with citations, and pre-generation guards refuse when retrieval cannot cite. The citation or the refusal is sealed in the trace for every answer.

  • How is voice latency handled — customers hang up on robots?

    Voice runs a barge-in-tuned path with a latency budget per turn; the console shows the budget against actuals, and a breach is a launch gate, not a tuning note after go-live.

  • Who fixes the dialog when intents drift?

    Drift is owned: the intent taxonomy and policy repo have named owners and a drift watch on the console. Policy changes are versioned like code, and the quarterly review retires intents nobody asks.
