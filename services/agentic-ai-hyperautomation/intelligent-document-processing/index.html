The queue is the product — and it is measured like one

Age bands, root causes and the shrinkage discipline that closes the backlog

Intelligent Document Processing is delivered by an Achala practice team that stays through industrialization. Achala builds this as production infrastructure, not a pilot: every agent is versioned, every permission is scoped, every handoff to a person is logged. The result is automation your operations and compliance teams can both stand behind. Who this is for: COOs and operations leads carrying process cost, and the risk officers who must approve what the agents touch.

The measure of success is the human queue, and it must shrink on measurement: straight-through rate climbs as the adjudicated eval set grows, and every correction a reviewer makes becomes training signal under version control.

Below: the failure modes that keep document queues eternal, the architecture that wires adjudication into the process, and the console that shows the queue shrinking by root cause.

Unrouted confidence to unowned aging — why document queues stay eternal

Unrouted confidence, unproven posting, uncaptured corrections, unowned aging

Each of these survives a pilot and bankrupts the operation at scale, the countermeasures target root causes so the queue shrinks instead of being staffed.

Adjudication wired into the process, not beside it

Extraction, confidence gates, reconciliation and the correction loop as one flow

Confidence gates route fields, not documents: high-confidence fields post, low-confidence fields route to an adjudication surface whose verdicts feed the extractor, so every human minute makes the next hundred documents cheaper.

The confidence gate is the product

Route-don't-guess adjudication, and why correction capture is the compounding asset

Document processing programs fail by optimizing extraction accuracy while ignoring the routing decision that actually protects the money path. The gate is the product: every extracted field carries a confidence score, and the gate routes on the score band, high confidence posts straight through after reconciliation, mid band goes to a human queue with the uncertain fields highlighted, low band goes back for recapture. The bands are set per document class from the risk matrix, because an invoice under $200 and a change order under $2M do not share a tolerance. Reconciliation against source, three-way match for PO flows, ledger match for accruals, runs before posting, so the queue measures exceptions, not errors.

Correction capture is the compounding asset. Every human fix is recorded as a labeled correction bound to the page region and the field, and the correction stream feeds the monthly gate recalibration, which is why straight-through share climbs for months after go-live instead of decaying. The queue itself is managed as a product: age bands, root-cause tags and a monthly shrinkage review that kills the worst extraction failure class each cycle, rather than a support inbox that quietly grows.

  • Route, do not guess

    Confidence bands set per class from the risk matrix; nothing posts uncertain.

  • Reconcile before post

    Three-way match on PO flows, ledger match on accruals, exceptions, not errors.

  • Correction capture

    Every fix bound to page region and field; feeds monthly gate recalibration.

  • Queue as a product

    Age bands, root-cause tags and a monthly shrinkage review with owners.

  • What happens when extraction confidence is low?

    The gate routes by confidence band: high posts automatically with page-level evidence, mid goes to review with fields pre-annotated, low routes to a human with the reason attached. The gate never silently guesses.

  • Can you prove what was posted matches the document?

    Every posted record links to its source page and field-level evidence. Maker-checker runs on the money path, and the audit extract reconstructs any posting end to end.

  • Does this work with our scanners, portals and EDI feeds?

    Yes, capture channels are typed inputs feeding one adjudication flow, and the systems of record receive typed, gated output rather than raw extractions.

  • How do human corrections make the system better?

    Every correction is captured with its reason and feeds band calibration. Correction velocity is a console metric, and the monthly review kills the worst failure class each cycle.

  • How long until the queue actually shrinks?

    The chart is the commitment: root-cause review from month one and straight-through targets signed per month. The month-6 band is the contract, not the aspiration.
